The plant is expected to save about ₹15 lakh monthly (≈₹1.8 crore annualized), has an estimated 25-year life, and will reduce reliance on grid power, lower energy costs, improve margins, and cut carbon emissions—supporting the company’s ESG and clean-energy transition objectives.
